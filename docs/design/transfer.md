# Transfer

Current source anchors are against HEAD `278436378c6c8013ee3f48e7817907c8adcf247e`.

## Decisions

1. **Spool layout + manifest.** The shared spool is rooted at `Transfers/` with `staging/`, `queued/`, `attention/`, `delete-sink/`, and `salvage/` directories, plus `manifest.json` and optional `body.upload` per item (`Sources/Transfer/TransferSpool.swift:108`). A `TransferManifest` carries the durable item contract: schema, item ID, source, created time, priority inputs, payload descriptors, endpoint descriptor, optional observer-ingest metadata, metadata JSON, disk state, retry deadline, save/start state, attention info, and app version (`Sources/Transfer/TransferManifest.swift:10`). Payload descriptors carry relative paths, filenames, content types, byte counts, content hashes, and whether a declared part is required for dispatch (`Sources/Transfer/TransferManifest.swift:177`).

2. **Outcome taxonomy.** HTTP results classify into terminal success, terminal attention, transient retry, or save-then-start continuation (`Sources/Transfer/TransferOutcome.swift:34`, `Sources/Transfer/TransferOutcome.swift:94`). Delivered outcomes remove the queued item after the send completes (`Sources/Transfer/TransferEngine.swift:742`). Attention outcomes move the item to `attention/` and clear retry state (`Sources/Transfer/TransferEngine.swift:769`, `Sources/Transfer/TransferSpool.swift:315`). Transient is never terminal: transient outcomes persist `nextAttemptAt`, keep the item queued, and emit `.retrying` (`Sources/Transfer/TransferEngine.swift:775`). The two `ImportQueue.swift` comments are intentional rationale anchors for behavior not carried over from the retired path: cancelled SAVEs now use uniform transient backoff (`Sources/Transfer/TransferOutcome.swift:100`) and mismatched SAVE echo no longer creates terminal failure (`Sources/Transfer/TransferOutcome.swift:154`).

3. **Scheduling, priority bands, and pacing.** `TransferEngine` is the single actor that owns queued, attention, in-flight, attempt, endpoint-held, and status state (`Sources/Transfer/TransferEngine.swift:173`). `scheduleWork()` coalesces drain passes and remains gated only by manual pause (`Sources/Transfer/TransferEngine.swift:575`). `drainEligibleWork()` consults dispatch policy for every dispatch decision, applies policy pause separately from manual pause, enforces the current lane cap, sleeps through the injected transfer clock for inter-item pacing, and re-checks policy after that suspension (`Sources/Transfer/TransferEngine.swift:585`). Eligibility is by priority band, source round-robin, retry deadline, payload presence, and endpoint availability (`Sources/Transfer/TransferEngine.swift:620`). Retry backoff is owned by `TransferPacer` (`Sources/Transfer/TransferPacer.swift:28`, `Sources/Transfer/TransferPacer.swift:64`). Live pacing numbers are owned only by `TransferDispatchPolicyDefaults`; the policy maps thermal, low-power, constrained, expensive, and finish-syncing mode into a dispatch decision (`Sources/Transfer/TransferDispatchPolicy.swift:24`, `Sources/Transfer/TransferDispatchPolicy.swift:39`).

4. **Crash-safety.** Producers stage payloads and a queued manifest before committing by directory move into `queued/` (`Sources/Transfer/TransferSpool.swift:213`, `Sources/Transfer/TransferSpool.swift:249`, `Sources/Transfer/TransferSpool.swift:292`). Manifest updates are atomic temp-write plus replace/move (`Sources/Transfer/TransferSpool.swift:302`). Startup scans queued and attention items, moves missing-required-payload items to attention, promotes complete staged items, and salvages incomplete or duplicate staged directories (`Sources/Transfer/TransferSpool.swift:158`, `Sources/Transfer/TransferSpool.swift:445`, `Sources/Transfer/TransferSpool.swift:488`). Delivered items move through `delete-sink/` before removal (`Sources/Transfer/TransferSpool.swift:372`). Delivered hooks are best-effort, at-most-once after delivery commit, and cannot mutate engine state (`Sources/Transfer/TransferEngine.swift:421`, `Sources/Transfer/TransferEngine.swift:764`).

5. **Per-source adapters.** Mobile segments enqueue finalized pending directories into transfer and remove the producer directory only after enqueue succeeds (`Sources/MobileSegment/MobileSegmentUploader.swift:1206`, `Sources/MobileSegment/MobileSegmentUploader.swift:1281`). Omi and Watch use `ObserverAudioTransferEnqueuer` to convert chunks/segments into manifests and payloads (`Sources/Observer/ObserverAudioTransfer.swift:100`, `Sources/Omi/OmiSegmentWriter.swift:204`, `Sources/WatchCapture/WatchSegmentDrain.swift:113`). Share imports stage in `ShareImportStore`, adopt pending/failed directories into transfer, and remove the share directory only after transfer enqueue succeeds (`Sources/ShareImport/ShareImportStore.swift:202`, `Sources/ShareImport/ShareImportTransferAdoption.swift:71`, `Sources/ShareImport/ShareImportTransferAdoption.swift:177`). One `TransferStatusMirror` is shared by the engine and all four holders (`Sources/SolstoneSwiftApp.swift:249`, `Sources/SolstoneSwiftApp.swift:264`, `Sources/SolstoneSwiftApp.swift:299`, `Sources/SolstoneSwiftApp.swift:317`, `Sources/SolstoneSwiftApp.swift:330`, `Sources/SolstoneSwiftApp.swift:365`); the mirror applies in-memory snapshots, including manual pause, policy pause, backoff, endpoint-held, per-source, and throughput fields (`Sources/Transfer/TransferStatusMirror.swift:67`, `Sources/Transfer/TransferStatusMirror.swift:82`).

6. **Deliberate constraints.** Transfer uses an in-process ephemeral `URLSession` upload transport, not a background session (`Sources/Transfer/TransferTransport.swift:8`, `Sources/Transfer/TransferTransport.swift:12`, `Sources/Transfer/TransferTransport.swift:71`). Delivery is at-least-once locally; server content-hash or idempotency contracts absorb duplicate sends, while local transient failures remain queued with backoff (`Sources/Transfer/TransferOutcome.swift:154`, `Sources/Transfer/TransferEngine.swift:775`). Share extension work stays extension-local until the app adopts it into the shared transfer spool (`Sources/ShareImport/ShareImportTransferAdoption.swift:71`, `Sources/ShareImport/ShareImportTransferAdoption.swift:181`). Background and finish-syncing drains read backoff status from the single mirror, and the shared stall decision treats live-endpoint backoff as progress rather than terminal stall (`Sources/UploadReach.swift:67`, `Sources/Background/TransferDrainProgress.swift:21`, `Sources/SolstoneSwiftApp.swift:465`, `Sources/SolstoneSwiftApp.swift:738`).
