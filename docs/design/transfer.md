# Transfer

Current source anchors are against the linked-device ingest v3 cutover.

## Decisions

1. **Spool layout + manifest.** The shared spool is rooted at `Transfers/` with `staging/`, `queued/`, `attention/`, `delete-sink/`, and `salvage/` directories, plus `manifest.json` and optional `body.upload` per item (`Sources/Transfer/TransferSpool.swift:182`). Observer manifests persist their linked-device ingest protocol version; predecessor observer bodies are invalidated before dispatch. Endpoint descriptors carry destination paths only—there is no persisted transfer auth flag (`Sources/Transfer/TransferManifest.swift:10`). Payload descriptors drive every multipart file part by filename and content type (`Sources/Transfer/TransferManifest.swift:179`).

2. **Outcome taxonomy.** HTTP results classify into terminal success, terminal attention, transient retry, or save-then-start continuation. Linked-device ingest delivers only when a 2xx JSON object has `status` `ok`, `duplicate`, or `collision`; malformed successful responses and `failed`/`conflict` stay durable in attention. Server errors retry with backoff (`Sources/Transfer/TransferOutcome.swift:103`).

3. **Scheduling, priority bands, and pacing.** `TransferEngine` is the single actor that owns queued, attention, in-flight, attempt, endpoint-held, and status state (`Sources/Transfer/TransferEngine.swift:199`). `scheduleWork()` coalesces drain passes and remains gated only by manual pause (`Sources/Transfer/TransferEngine.swift:602`). `drainEligibleWork()` consults dispatch policy for every dispatch decision, applies policy pause separately from manual pause, enforces the current lane cap, sleeps through the injected transfer clock for inter-item pacing, and re-checks policy after that suspension (`Sources/Transfer/TransferEngine.swift:612`, `Sources/Transfer/TransferEngine.swift:622`). Eligibility is by priority band, source round-robin, retry deadline, payload presence, and endpoint availability (`Sources/Transfer/TransferEngine.swift:654`). Retry backoff is owned by `TransferPacer` (`Sources/Transfer/TransferPacer.swift:28`, `Sources/Transfer/TransferPacer.swift:64`). Live pacing numbers are owned only by `TransferDispatchPolicyDefaults`; the policy maps thermal, low-power, constrained, expensive, and finish-syncing mode into a dispatch decision (`Sources/Transfer/TransferDispatchPolicy.swift:24`, `Sources/Transfer/TransferDispatchPolicy.swift:39`).

4. **Crash-safety.** Producers stage payloads and a queued manifest before committing by directory move into `queued/`. Manifest updates are atomic. Startup and attention requeue normalize predecessor observer items by deleting a stale body cache before atomically stamping v3; a crash before the rewrite repeats safely on relaunch (`Sources/Transfer/TransferSpool.swift:232`). Delivered items move through `delete-sink/` before removal, and delivered hooks remain best-effort and at-most-once after delivery commit.

5. **Per-source adapters.** Mobile, Omi, and Watch enqueue descriptor-backed observer items with a durable source discriminator; location and screencast ride in `mobile-segment` items. Reconciliation uses a single linked-device client with explicit `source` query parameters and remains display-only. Share imports retain their separate SAVE body, carrying no registration or observer handle beyond the paired tunnel.

6. **Deliberate constraints.** Transfer uses an in-process ephemeral `URLSession` upload transport. Linked-device ingest is keyless over the paired tunnel and always sends protocol header `3`; its envelope is the authority contract. Share extension work remains extension-local until app adoption and its SAVE flow carries no client credential of its own.
